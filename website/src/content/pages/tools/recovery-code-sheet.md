## Why print backup codes

Backup codes (also called recovery codes) are what get you into an account when your phone or authenticator app is gone. They exist for the bad day, so the place you keep them has to survive that day too. A sheet of paper doesn't need a charged phone, a working laptop or a password to read.

Each code works once. Ticking them off as you use them tells you how many are left, before you run out.

## How to use it

1. On the site, open its two-factor or security settings and choose to show or make backup codes.
2. Copy them and paste them into the box above, one per line. Add the site's name and the account you sign in with.
3. Check the preview, then click **Print the sheet**. Only the sheet prints.
4. Close this tab. Nothing was saved.

Write the sheet's place down somewhere you'll remember, like "with the passports", and tell one person you trust.
