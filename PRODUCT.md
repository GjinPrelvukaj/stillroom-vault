# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People on a Mac who keep things that would hurt them if they got out: scans of passports and contracts, tax papers,
passwords and two-factor setups, private notes, evidence they are gathering. Some are professionals (lawyers, clinics,
journalists); some are people protecting themselves from someone close to them. They arrive wary: they have seen
cloud breaches and vague security marketing, and they read the small print. The job: put private things in one place
that only they can open, without trusting a company's server.

## Product Purpose

Stillroom Vault is a local-only encrypted vault for macOS: files, notes, passwords and two-factor codes sealed with
AES-256-GCM under keys only the owner holds. No server, no account, no sync. The website's job is to make that
promise felt in seconds and then proven in plain words: the threat model, the public file format, the offline check.
Success is a visitor who trusts it enough to download the free version (25 items) and later pays once ($39).

## Brand Personality

Calm, exact, unhurried. A quiet room with a lock on the door. The voice is plain and specific, says what it does not
do as readily as what it does, and never hypes. Emotional arc on the home page: awe in the first seconds (something
being sealed, big and slow), then calm and clarity, like the site's article pages.

## Anti-references

- AI-purple glow: blurred violet blobs, gradient text, neon outer glows, mesh-gradient heroes.
- Glassmorphism used as decoration; identical icon-card grids; the SaaS hero-metric template.
- An Apple-product-page clone (tried and rejected): borrowed restraint is fine, a copied look is not.
- "Hacker" cliché: matrix rain, green-on-black terminals as decoration, hex textures, padlock clip-art.
- Security marketing language: military-grade, bulletproof, unbreakable, peace of mind.

## Design Principles

1. Practice what we preach: nothing loaded from anyone else, no tracking, works without JavaScript; the page itself is
   evidence.
2. Show, then tell: let the visitor watch a file being sealed, and seal their own sentence, before explaining it.
3. Honest limits are part of the design: what it doesn't protect against sits beside what it does.
4. Calm over clever: one strong moment, then quiet reading. Motion has a reason or it goes.
5. One accent, used for meaning: violet marks what is sealed, lit or chosen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA everywhere (axe clean, Lighthouse accessibility 100). Every animation has a still version under
prefers-reduced-motion, and every page works without JavaScript. Keyboard reachable throughout, visible focus,
contrast at least 4.5:1 for text on the near-black ground. No information conveyed by colour alone.
